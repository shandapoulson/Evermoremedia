FAQ

Good questions.

Don't see yours? Email us at shanda@itsevermoremedia.com.

Is there a contract?

Yes. We start with a six-month minimum, then go month to month from there. Social media and marketing build momentum over time, and six months gives us enough time for you to see the true benefit of our work.

Do I need to be local?

Not at all. We work with businesses anywhere in the US. In-person content shoots are available in the Asheville and Folsom/Greater Sacramento areas. Everywhere else, you supply the content and we supply the ideas, shot lists and examples.

Do I get to approve content before it posts?

Always. Twice a month we send your content calendar with the posts and captions for your approval. Nothing goes live until you've signed off.

What if I don't have branding yet?

We follow your existing branding. If you don't have any, we'll set up simple brand basics, like colors, fonts and templates, so your social and email look cohesive.

How do paid Meta ads work?

Paid ads are an add-on to any package. Ad management is an additional monthly fee, and your ad spend is a separate budget you pay directly to Meta, so you always control how much goes into ads.

Can I add services later?

Yes. Add-ons like expanded email marketing, Pinterest management, SEO and AI-search blog posts, and paid Meta ads can be added to any package whenever you're ready.

Fill out our inquiry form. We'll set up a quick discovery call to talk through your goals, then put together a custom plan built around your business.
