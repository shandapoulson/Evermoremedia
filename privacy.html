Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Evermore Media (“Evermore,” “we,” “us”) is a social media and marketing studio based in Asheville, North Carolina and Folsom, California. This policy explains what information we collect through itsevermoremedia.com, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information you give us

We collect information you choose to share with us, including:

  • Inquiry form: your name, email, phone number, business name, website or social handle, industry, location, the services you’re interested in, budget, timeline, goals and how you heard about us.
  • Email signup: your email address, if you join our list.
  • Emails and messages: anything you send us directly.

Information collected automatically

  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and running well.
  • Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • The site saves a small note in your browser (local storage) to remember whether you’ve seen or closed our email signup pop-up. It isn’t used to track you.

We don’t use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this site.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiry and talk with you about our services
  • To provide services to clients
  • To send our email newsletter, if you signed up. Every email includes a link to unsubscribe.
  • To keep the site secure and improve it
  • To meet legal obligations

How we share information

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t share it for advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as:

  • Formspree, which delivers form submissions to our inbox
  • Vercel, which hosts this website
  • Google, which provides our fonts and business email
  • Our email marketing provider, which sends our newsletter

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the reasons above, or as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

  • Access, correct or delete: email us at shanda@itsevermoremedia.com and we’ll help.
  • Unsubscribe: use the link at the bottom of any of our emails, or email us.
  • Do Not Track: we don’t track visitors across other websites, so we don’t respond differently to Do Not Track signals.

Children

This site is meant for businesses and isn’t directed to children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no website or email transmission is completely secure.

Links to other sites

Our site links to other sites, such as Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. Their privacy practices are their own, so please review their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Email us at shanda@itsevermoremedia.com.
